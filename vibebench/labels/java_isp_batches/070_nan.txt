      197     * <p>
      198     * <b>Two-phase flow with lock release during replication:</b>
      199     * <ol>
      200     *   <li><b>Phase 1 (read lock held):</b> Capture WAL bytes and bucket record deltas
      201     *       via {@code commit1stPhase}. The read lock ensures a consistent snapshot of the
      202     *       transaction's page changes.</li>
