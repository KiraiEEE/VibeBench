      112      """
      113      Improvers are responsible to improve already imported data by an importer.  An improver is
      114      required to override the ``interesting_advisories`` property method to return a QuerySet of
      115      ``Advisory`` objects. These advisories are then passed to ``get_inferences`` method which is
      116      responsible for returning an iterable of ``Inferences`` for that particular ``Advisory``
      117      """
