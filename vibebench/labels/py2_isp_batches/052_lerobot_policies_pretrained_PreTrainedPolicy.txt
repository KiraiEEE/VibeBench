      108            - `n_obs_steps` steps worth of observations are cached (for the first steps, the observation is
      109              copied `n_obs_steps` times to fill the cache).

      114              (legend: o = n_obs_steps, h = horizon, a = n_action_steps)

      116              |observation is used | YES   | YES   | YES   | YES   | NO    | NO    | NO    | NO    | NO    |
      117              |action is generated | YES   | YES   | YES   | YES   | YES   | YES   | YES   | YES   | YES   |
      118              |action is used      | NO    | NO    | NO    | YES   | YES   | YES   | NO    | NO    | NO    |
      119              ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
