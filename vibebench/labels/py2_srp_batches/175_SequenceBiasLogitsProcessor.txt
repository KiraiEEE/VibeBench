     1207  class SequenceBiasLogitsProcessor(LogitsProcessor):
     1208      """
     1209      [`LogitsProcessor`] that applies an additive bias on sequences. The bias is applied to the last token of a sequence
     1210      when the next generated token can complete it. Consequently, to take the most of biasing sequences with more than
     1211      one token, consider using beam methods (to gracefully work around partially completed sequences that have a
     1212      negative bias) and applying the bias to their prefixes (to ensure the bias is applied earlier).
     1213  
     1214      <Tip>
     1215  
     1216      At a token-level, biasing a word is different from biasing a word with a space before it. If you want to bias
     1217      "foo" mid-sentence, you'll likely want to add a prefix space and bias " foo" instead. Check the tokenizer section
     1218      of our NLP course to find out why: https://huggingface.co/learn/nlp-course/chapter2/4?fw=pt
     1219  
     1220      </Tip>
     1221  
     1222      Args:
     1223          sequence_bias (`list[list[Union[list[int], float]]]`):
     1224              List of lists that maps a sequence of tokens to its bias term (e.g. `[[[10, 45], -2.0],
     1225              [[64], -7.5]]`). Positive biases increase the odds of the
     1226              sequence being selected, while negative biases do the opposite. If a sequence has a length of 1, its bias
     1227              will always be applied. Otherwise, the bias will only be applied if the sequence in question is about to be
     1228              completed (in the token selection step after this processor is applied).
